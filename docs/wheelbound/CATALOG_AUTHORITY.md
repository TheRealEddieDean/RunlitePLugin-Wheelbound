## Normative gaps carried forward

WB-D101 requires a finite 100-target item system including grouped any-third-age and any-gilded families. Those families are absent from the experimental 100-item list. A grouped family consumes one target and settles once through a qualifying canonical acquisition receipt; ownership, purchase or transforming an existing item is insufficient. Adding families must replace/review targets in a new 100-target version, not silently expand the count or rewrite H3 history.

WB-D035 approves dynamic punishment architecture, not each entry of this new draft. The historical fifty-template list was deferred at S8-M0172; an approved complete historical list has not been recovered in the review. The draft's 120-minute generation cap is experimental. WB-D087 permits feasible multi-day escalation for consecutive Penance; clean assigned completion resets the sequence. No automatic third-offense level-99 task is confirmed. Review plain, method/location-restricted, and escalated templates independently.

The broad Challenge task library and ordinary-slice classification need explicit versioned contracts. Additional Bossing Challenge eligibility is Q9; it stays disabled pending resolution. Temporary or exhausted ordinary pools are Q6; do not assign completed quests or invent automatic free wheel edits. Rejected-Fate bounty disposition is Q10; do not silently erase receipts or claw back FP.

## Promotion and verification

Promotion requires source/access evidence, objective feasibility, positive and negative receipt fixtures, paused/relogged/account-swap behavior, idempotent settlement, and the relevant policy gate. Source inspection is distinct from live validation. Update affected specifications and DECISIONS when a new mechanic is authorized; routine catalog review does not invent prior approval. Persist a new manifest version and immutable model inputs before executing a changed simulation.
