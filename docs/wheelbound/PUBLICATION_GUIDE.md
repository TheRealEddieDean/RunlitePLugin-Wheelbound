## Exact meaning of the four ranks

For the unmultiplied per-roll probability p, use its exact reciprocal D. Lesser: 25 <= D <= 50; Standard: 50 < D <= 125; Greater: 125 < D <= 750; Superior: 750 < D <= 5000. Shared endpoints belong to the easier rank. Roll counts and personal encounter probability remain separate evidence; a per-roll tier is not automatically a per-kill rate. Rank rarity does not order task duration.

Rank ownership is paid and progressive Lesser to Standard to Greater to Superior, retaining lower ranks. Higher purchases start disabled unless the player explicitly enables them. A ready rank still needs at least three actually eligible canonical creature keys after ownership, enabled-source and supported access checks. Unknown predicates remain unsupported. Multiple drops from one creature do not create extra creature diversity or extra creature weight.

Hunts require the fresh personal assigned-source drop while active; keep the item and settle once. Previous possession does not exclude a hunt. No KC cap, pity, general reroll or reward repricing is introduced. Freeze the source context and quote. Ordinary Bossing Lesser/Standard/Greater are distinct from these Challenge rank names, and initial Lesser retains its established ordinary Bossing entry gate.
