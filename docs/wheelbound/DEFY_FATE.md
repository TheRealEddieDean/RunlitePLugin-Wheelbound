Actual bank snapshot may be stale/incomplete; do not assume API sees unopened bank.

If no genuinely eligible item exists, the player must acquire an eligible item worth at least 10,000 GP and sacrifice it. This includes a genuinely empty unprotected candidate set. Bank unavailable and selected item lost still require recovery handling; pause remains free. Acquisition is required, but purchasing is not the only permitted source.

## Forced-sacrifice evidence and acquisition recovery
Trigger commits obligation ID, blessings and known ownership. Candidate identity/quantity/value persists before display. Opening a bank may refresh unknown data; unknown is never equivalent to empty. Selection locks the selected unit. Donation must correlate selected item/quantity, accepted Coffer interaction, container decrease and actual receipt/value signal. A click or item disappearance is insufficient. Account/session mismatch prevents credit.

WB-D058 confirms one spin for a Taint task, with no second charge for the resulting Punishment. Proposed transaction timing: debit once when the Taint outcome and obligation commit; persist the receipt across restarts. Sacrifice also consumes exactly one spin (explicit user approval, 2026-10-09). H1 and the prior all-special-debit sensitivity predate this approval and are not final balance evidence.

## Acquisition recovery specification
Confirmed states: pending forced Sacrifice -> no eligible candidate -> acquire eligible item -> validate/freeze candidate -> verified donation -> award spins -> ordinary progression available. The obligation is never cleared by acquiring the item alone. Unknown bank evidence must be refreshed before declaring an empty set.

Proposed implementation detail: treat acquisition as a recovery objective within the same obligation. Show an observable legal method using existing skill, region, shop and equipment access; do not silently waive access gates. Attribute only progress needed for the selected acquisition route as permitted recovery activity; freeze the route before tracking to prevent arbitrary off-task training. The exact allowed method and incidental-XP map require content validation, not a blanket tolerance exemption. Changing/losing the candidate returns to recovery without another landing or spin charge. Free Pause remains available and excludes all paused credit.

After Sacrifice lands, debit one spin exactly once, then award converted spins only on verified donation. A zero balance while this obligation is pending does not force another Defy before recovery; the current obligation must be settled first. This ordering is the proposed transaction interpretation of the confirmed costs. Voluntary donation does not land on a task and receives no new debit from WB-D058.
