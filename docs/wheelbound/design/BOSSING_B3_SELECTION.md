# Bossing Wheel — B3 Ancient Stone (simplified)

Status: USER SELECTED visual direction, 2026-10-27. Design reference only; no runtime implementation approved.

The user chose the **left-hand B3 Ancient Stone wheel** from the latest simplified B3/B5 comparison in the design conversation. This is **not** the ornate first B3 concept.

## Selected look
- Simple dark-gray segmented stone rim, with **four blue gemstone markers** at cardinal positions.
- Muted dark brown/charcoal wedge backgrounds, thin dark separators.
- Minimal decorations: no glowing rune inscriptions, no elaborate chains, skull perimeter, spikes, or ornate gold trim.
- Restrained OSRS/RuneLite pixel-art visual language, readable labels and small icons.
- Simple dark central hub with a small blue compass/star detail; small upper pointer.
- Distinct from Grand Fate GF4 (red wedges, iron chain ring, skulls and spikes), and from the unchanged existing Master wheel.

The last comparison image showed example boss **categories** and is illustrative only. Actual Bossing Fate selection remains governed by approved eligible-individual-boss rules, not the mockup's example categories. Do not bake sample labels or icon art into production.
