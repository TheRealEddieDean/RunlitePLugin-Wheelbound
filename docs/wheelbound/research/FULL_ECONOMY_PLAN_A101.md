## A concrete spending comparison

A100's middle rank ladder costs3,750 FP. Its500FP ordinary-card prerequisite proxy plus nine hypothetical150FP expansions makes5,600FP before other purchases. The model's nine are three each for Standard/Greater/Superior, not four paid Lesser bundle targets. Its current strategy buys those before moving on; that is one simulated preference, not an approved shop ordering rule.

| Existing candidate cost | Compact ranks | Middle ranks | High ranks |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Four ranks only | 2,250 | 3,750 | 6,000 |
| Plus500 card proxy + nine150 expansions | 4,100 | 5,600 | 7,850 |
| Same, two target charges avoided through hypothetical ownership reuse | 3,800 | 5,300 | 7,550 |
| No reuse, plus third offer1,000 and GE2,500 | 7,600 | 9,100 | 11,350 |

These are FP budget sums, not completion counts or evidence that an account can access the targets. GE also has a completion gate; the price alone does not unlock it. Actual boss tiers/tree purchases and equipment/supply access are omitted from these totals, so they are not full costs to reach Superior. A100's arbitrary discretionary percentage cannot explain which concrete purchases an account made. Replace that proxy with named purchase policies in the next model.

[Thirty-case arithmetic packet](../../../automation/challenge_ranks/economy_visible/BUDGET_PLAN_A101.json) covers three ladders × two reuse assumptions × five competing carts. Every total is recomputed and reuse reduces matching sums by300. The two-key branch is sensitivity only: Commander Zilyana and K'ril are identifiable normal-boss overlap leads, but an explicit reviewed normal-node→Challenge-key map and actual ownership are required before any reuse. Never infer it from labels, grant normal access with a Challenge purchase or charge twice for an approved reused entitlement. No concrete mapping is approved by this packet.

Do not use synthetic tests as current-client proof.
5. **A106: Bounded scenario batches (P1/M each).** First check a small deterministic cohort, then expand only after invariants pass. Compare baseline, ordinary unlock rush, Challenge rush, mixed spending and no-bounty strategies, with explicit access profiles and both two/three offerings. Report seeds, input hashes, unfinished tasks, rank ownership/readiness, debt, FP income/spend by category, elapsed modeled hours and spin journal. Never collapse results to a claimed real playthrough duration.
6. **A107: Balance recommendation packet (P1/S).** Propose a coherent schedule only after comparing tradeoffs and identifying omitted mechanics. Adopt context-supported routine recommendations as DELEGATED; preserve locked rules and raise one material ambiguity only when it actually blocks a chosen deliverable.
