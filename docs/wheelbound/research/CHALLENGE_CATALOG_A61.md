## Personal probability contexts

Single-opportunity source fractions are per specific awarded item in a complete solo kill. Solo-only means no other player's damage/ownership allocation in that encounter; a team loot chance is not divided by team size. Grouped forms do not add entries. God Wars uses normal boss source IDs and ordinary renewable faction-KC entry, excluding finite-key-only entry, bodyguard drops and team allocation. Corp's captured prose identifies top-damage loot ownership; authoring solo removes recipient competition. Phosani is inherently solo; no ordinary Nightmare context substituted.

Zulrah's three selected uniques report two 1/1024 rolls. Let A and B be the specific-item outcomes. Regardless of dependence, max(P(A),P(B)) ≤ P(A∪B) ≤ min(1,P(A)+P(B)); hence the published-source model gives **[1/1024,1/512]**. This entire conservative range is inside [1/1500,1/400]. No independent-roll or additive exact per-kill rate is asserted. The source's approximately 1/512 narrative is preserved as context, not used to erase the roll structure. Reported table fractions are source-model inputs; these are not empirical confidence intervals or newly measured game probabilities.

Vorkath's necklace is a single-roll **tertiary**; its two main-table rolls never multiply the necklace chance. Tormented burning claw uses exact reported 499/250000, not a rounded 1/500. Phosani staff/mace rates preserve 69/35000 and 31/35000. Heavy ballista frame and blood shard include exact 1/1500 endpoint. All selected probability bounds pass exact rational inclusion.

## Conditions resolved for this version

- **Task-only or task-boosted rare contexts:** excluded v1. Kraken, Cerberus, Smoke devil, Alchemical Hydra, superiors and task-only Basilisk/Drake/Hydra alternatives do not enter the selectable table. An uncapped hunt cannot rely on a finite remaining assignment; no free task, task hunting permission, off-task credit or exhaustion reroll is invented. Task-dependent expansion remains independently pending for a later version.
- **Group/raid/contribution/stateful contexts:** Nex, raids, ordinary Nightmare, multi-recipient Wilderness bosses and DT2 vestige progress models are excluded v1. No unconditional party rate or long-run hidden-progress fraction is passed off as a personal independent encounter chance.
- **Wilderness singles:** Artio/Spindel/Calvar'ion require permanent hard-diary access and an existing Wilderness permission, with renewable entry-fee funding. The finite boss-task bypass is not used. Solo-only complete encounters; normal larger variants and minion drops cannot substitute. Both Calvar'ion combat phases share one key and one completed opportunity.
- **Source-family uniqueness:** signature components/upgrades shared by a narrow related source family (dragon limbs, whip, blood shard, ring/weapon components) can be authored for one exact assigned creature. Other sources never complete that hunt. Generic loot/shared RDT, godsword shards, jars, heads, pets and shop-equipment families are not selected. Low GP price does not remove meaningful equipment/components.
- **Variants:** black mask means newly dropped Black mask (10), not a transformation of an old mask. Only explicit dropped IDs count; charged/ornamented/noted alternatives need a later version. Prefix matching is forbidden because RuneLite constants include pets, quest actors and alternate modes. Pinned IDs prove symbolic identity, not live detector behavior. Post-quest/normal-world/region/encounter predicates are mandatory even when IDs overlap.
