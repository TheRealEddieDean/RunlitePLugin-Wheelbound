# Content acceptance and rollout

Status: DELEGATED release process; no content is claimed live-verified. The newly authored catalogs do not replace missing historical approvals invisibly.

## Item bounties
Every enabled entry needs canonical item IDs (including correct note/charged/ornament handling), legitimate source NPC/chest IDs, owner allocation, acquisition receipt, duplicate-event key and current rarity/reward review. ItemID comments verify identity only. A random inventory increase, collection-log history or buying an item cannot earn the bounty. The draft source family narrows research, not an exhaustive drop table. For raids/chests, partial reward UI, bank delivery, duplicate container notifications and interrupted claim are distinct tests.

Candidate families: 24 Barrows, 16 God Wars, 12 Chambers, seven Theatre, seven Tombs, three Zulrah, four Cerberus, four Dagannoth rings, eight mixed Slayer/PvM, eight Wilderness, three Gauntlet and four Corp. The mixed family includes Sarachnis as a PvM source, not a Slayer-only restriction. Source-based adapter boundaries must be reviewed per entry. This content mix is a new design draft, not the lost original list.

Per-run permanent rewards are once-only. A supported active permitted completion automatically pays once under WB-D112. Pausing does not erase prior receipts/FP; it forbids new payout processing under WB-D114. Delayed/ambiguous receipts retain evidence for reviewed active settlement. Proposed overlap permanent/daily can pay once in each independently keyed ledger. No automatic payout simply because a panel opens.

Daily catalog must be separately authored using repeatable short-horizon legal acquisition routes. Do not sample the 100 rare permanent entries into dailies: a one-day rare-item chase can be infeasible. A daily board may be unavailable if three distinct legal targets cannot be constructed; it never blocks ordinary progress. Daily income is absent from current H3 and must not be represented as simulated.

## Punishments
Initial draft has 23 ordinary-XP, 12 method/tool, eight location-XP and seven owned-KC templates. A template's title does not prove a detector. Basic XP progress still needs initialization, pause/account guard and permitted by-products. Method templates require an authored positive receipt; KP/NPC deaths need ownership attribution and reviewed variants, not proximity. Position-only XP attribution requires a queue/context model and teleport/instance tests.

Before assignment, check current unlock, real level, access, resources and rate/headroom. Freeze required quantity and reward. Preserved H3 experimental duration bands are 30/60/90 minutes with a 120-minute planned cap, not measured playtime or the normative punishment ceiling. Recovered consecutive Penance permits feasible severe multi-day objectives; future faithful catalogs/models need a separate version. Resource-constrained players cannot be forced into unavailable rune/bar/tool acquisition. No arbitrary rare-drop completion gate or deliberate player death.

Eligible pool size can be less than twelve. Show the actual wheel, never duplicate outcomes to inflate it. A zero pool is a generation failure, not a violation. Preserve the obligation/rejection receipt and allow immediate free pause; repair the generator before another draw. No silent committed-outcome swap is approved. Runtime enablement requires current-client proof even for a template that worked in an old version.
